## Quasit [click to see monster](http://www.wizards.com/dnd/images/MM35_gallery/MM35_PG45.jpg)

### Combat

#### [Poison](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#poison) ([Ex](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#extraordinaryAbilities))

Injury, Fortitude DC 13, initial damage 1d4 Dex, secondary damage 2d4 Dex. The save DC is Constitution-based and includes a +2 racial bonus.

#### [Spell-Like Abilities](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#spellLikeAbilities)

At will—[detect good](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/detectGood.htm), [detect magic](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/detectMagic.htm), and [invisibility](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/invisibility.htm) (self only); 1/day—[cause fear](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/causeFear.htm) (as the spell, except that its area is a 30-foot radius from the quasit, save DC 11). Caster level 6th.

The save DCs are Charisma-based.

Once per week a quasit can use [commune](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/commune.htm) to ask six questions. The ability otherwise works as the spell (caster level 12th).

#### [Alternate Form](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#alternateForm) ([Su](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#supernaturalAbilities))

A quasit can assume another form at will as a [standard action](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/combat/actionsInCombat.htm#standardActions). Each quasit can assume one or two forms from the following list: [bat](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/bat.htm), Small or Medium [monstrous centipede](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/monstrousCentipede.htm), [toad](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/toad.htm), and [wolf](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/monsters/wolf.htm).

Although quasits thirst for victory and power as other demons do, they are cowards at heart. They typically attack from ambush, using their [alternate form](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#alternateForm) ability and [invisibility](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#invisibility) to get within reach, then try to scuttle away. When retreating, they use their [cause fear](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/causeFear.htm) ability to deter pursuit.

A quasit’s [natural weapons](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#naturalWeapons), as well as any weapons it wields, are treated as chaotic-aligned and evil-aligned for the purpose of overcoming [damage reduction](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/specialAbilities.htm#damageReduction).

Quasits speak Common and Abyssal.
